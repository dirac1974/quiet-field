How to use the field

One small loop. Keep it short.
  1. Pick a path. Gettysburg Address or the Preamble. More paths can join the field later.
  2. Look. Picture, hear the line, one why, one gesture.
  3. Echo. Hide the words. Say them. Mic is optional.
  4. Walk. The next lanterns in real speech order.
  5. Mix. Which known line comes first on the path?
